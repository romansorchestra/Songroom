## Most specific findings

1. **AA-F2:** On Song Exploder, Antonoff says the best artists know when to be funny because it makes the next sad line sadder. The bridge of Please Please Please puts a sincere, heartbreaking line straight before a line that undercuts it with a joke.
2. **AA-F4:** On NPR, Allen says the line or note that raises a "too weird / too personal / too niche" flag is usually the best part of the song, and she chases it. Her example is the profane word in Please Please Please. Critics of Man's Best Friend argue against this habit (overused profanity and innuendo).
3. **AA-F6:** Allen says McRae decided weeks later that the Greedy chorus lyric was wrong, and the song's concept was completely changed. Allen trusts the artist because the artist has to sing it for years.
4. **AA-F5:** For artists Allen always writes the chorus first and calls it the crux of the song. If someone wants to redo the chorus, she thinks they should write a new song. For her own album she wrote from the first word to the last.
5. **AA-F7:** Allen says she is bad at writing to a title someone hands her ("a Sudoku puzzle"). She finds the song by talking with the artist about their life.
