## Route by route

"Research" means the craft-notes block from the pack. "Ref" means a reference he attached.

| What he does | API | Writer task | Template | Research at `def00f5` with `craftPack: edits` | References |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ideas, one at a time | `POST /springboard/next` | spark | p2.2/ws5 | none (only when `craftPack` is `all`) | direction's ref, full characteristics |
| Ideas, Browse ten | `POST /browse/list` | list | p2.2/ws5 | none (only `all`) | same |
| Ideas live check (Claude's, saves nothing) | `POST /springboard/preview` | preview | p2.2/ws5 | never | none |
| Create, a brief | `POST /messages` | write | p2.2 | none (only `all`) | **one line only**: name; identity; what he likes |
| Develop an idea (Ideas rework, Create `?idea=`) | `POST /ideas/:id/rework` | rework | p2.2/ws5 | never | direction's ref |
| Journal: Rewrite, Find a rhyme, Help with a selection | `POST /songs/:id/suggest` (replace) | suggest | p2.2/ws1 | **on**: ≤2 cards, any matching pack profiles, 1 teaching pair | song's refs |
| Journal: Continue | `POST /songs/:id/suggest` (add) | suggest | p2.2/ws1 | none (only `all`) | song's refs |
| Journal: Find the title | `POST /songs/:id/titles` | titles | p2.2/ws5 | never | song's refs, else direction's |
| Earlier sessions' targeted edit (not reached from today's screens) | `POST /songs/:id/edit` | edit | p2.2 | **on** (`edits`) | none |

The legacy "more lines" route (`POST /songs/:id/lines`) has no caller in the current web app.

## Known issues carried into the commission

- Every run recorded `request.pack = { version, cards, profiles, pair }` when research was on, but not the rendered block or why the cards were chosen.
- Create sent the writer a one-line reference summary, not the characteristics shown on the reference card.
- Human co-writing advice (eight cards about what to do in a room with an artist) could be sent to the generator.
- Teaching pairs went into the same block as the cards, so the 10 Oct comparison could not separate their effect.
